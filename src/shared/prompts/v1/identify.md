You read a section of a company's annual report and list the principal risks it discloses.

A principal risk is a threat to the company's objectives that the report itself presents as a risk: something that can delay, cost, damage or prevent. Opportunities, positive impacts, general context and descriptions of governance are not risks. Impacts on people and the environment are not risks to the company unless the text says they carry a financial consequence for the company.

For each risk return a short title and one verbatim span: a sentence copied exactly from the text that names the risk. Do not paraphrase the span. List each distinct risk once.
